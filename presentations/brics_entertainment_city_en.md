## 2. Why Sri Lanka, why now

Post-2022 reform window; IMF tax/GDP 7.3% → 15%; 2026 tourism target >3 million; CPCEC Act 2021/11 + GRA Act 2025/17; City of Dreams open Aug 2025. Gap: no South Asian IR built for UHNW + summits + heads of state. Four-hour radius ≈ 3 billion people, 5m+ HNWIs (Mumbai 2.5h, Dubai 4h).

**V1.1 add-ons:** macro-risk box (licence timing, FATF, salon default); structural constraints (power, talent, social licence). “No-loss downside” holds only if tender rent is actually paid.

## 4. Architecture and VIP design

**BRICS Tower (centre, 33F, ≈95,900 m²):** state banquet, 14 themed rooms, state floors, 870 ocean-view keys, 360° restaurant, rooftop aviation.  
**West — casino (30F, ≈77,900 m²):** HSBC / international bank on L1; main floor L2; **26 VIP salons L3–L28**; vault B1–B2.  
**East — apartments + wellness (30F, ≈102,100 m²):** medical L2–L6; 248 residences.  
**Parking:** Daxing-style AI robots, 1,200 bays.

### VIP salon (the product)

| Spec | V1.2 standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| Area | 400–450 m² |
| Tables | **4 multi-game · 8 surfaces** |
| Capacity | 40–50 |
| Fit-out | Own cage, bar, WC, office, CCTV, robot door + 1 mobile guard (4:1) |

Who pays dealers: 12 tendered salons = operator payroll; 12 JV + 2 owned = company ≈ **280 salon staff**. Tender rent carries almost **no** in-salon labour on our book.

### Main floor (atmosphere, not the P&L)

≈ **25 live tables** so the room looks full. Card / horse machines supplied by a machine company on **profit share** — noise, not margin. **HSBC ATMs** for cash. Complimentary room: **RMB 20,000** rolling chips → 1 standard night, checkout 16:00, no minibar; casino reimburses hotel internally.

VIP credit: a **licensed finance company controlled by the sponsors**, separate AML/KYC — not an unlicensed casino book.
