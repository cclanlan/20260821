- Tourism rebound: 2026 target above 3 million international arrivals; India is the largest source market

- Precedent: City of Dreams opened August 2025, proving large international IR operations are executable in Port City
- Market gap: South Asia still lacks a world-class complex built around UHNW VIP, summit hosting, and head-of-state reception
- Geography: 4-hour flight radius covers ~3 billion people and 5+ million HNWIs; Mumbai 2.5 hours, Dubai 4 hours

Slide 5 — Four strategic positions (complementary to City of Dreams)

1. **South Asia’s UHNW VIP gaming destination** — rolling-chip tickets from US$250,000; 26 independent VIP salons; three-track operating model
2. **Flagship for international summits and diplomatic reception** — ends the practice of moving high-protocol events offshore for lack of venues
3. **South Asia regenerative medicine and anti-ageing flagship** — an independent medical-tourism funnel that lengthens stay and non-gaming spend
4. **A new Asian vault for UHNW assets** — Port City’s legally ring-fenced jurisdiction plus basement vaults, as an alternative to Singapore and Hong Kong

City of Dreams addresses mass-to-premium integrated resort demand. This project specialises in UHNW, diplomacy, wellness, and custody. Two world-class complexes in one district raise Port City’s category, rather than cannibalise it.

Slide 13 — Legal and compliance

- Statute: CPCEC Act No. 11 of 2021; GRA Act No. 17 of 2025
- Process: licence application under GRA procedure (fit-and-proper, financial capacity, compliance, responsible gaming)
- Precedent: Melco 20-year licence; City of Dreams opening in 2025
- Tax: 20% CIT concession contracted in the CPCEC authorised-person agreement
- Protection: BITs + ICSID; arbitration in Singapore or Hong Kong
- AML: FATF-aligned KYC/AML built from day one, in support of the national mutual evaluation
- Responsible gaming: access controls, problem-gambling intervention, enterprise compliance (Business Plan Ch. 10)
